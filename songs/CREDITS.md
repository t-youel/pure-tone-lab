# Songs

All songs in this folder are by **Josh Woodward** (https://www.joshwoodward.com), released under the
Creative Commons Attribution licence (CC BY). Each comes with its official instrumental version.

- Afterglow — Josh Woodward (CC BY)
- Coffee — Josh Woodward (CC BY)
- Crazy Glue — Josh Woodward (CC BY)
- I'm Letting Go — Josh Woodward (CC BY)

They are used to prepare songs for Learn from the pros: the song minus its instrumental leaves the singer's voice,
from which his notes and the timing of every word are measured and checked, then built into the app.
