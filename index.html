How purity is measured

Clarity is how strongly the sound repeats itself, cycle after cycle (normalized autocorrelation). A clean, periodic tone scores near 100. Air, rasp and crackle lower it.

Steadiness is how still your pitch stays over the last 0.4 seconds, measured in cents. Gentle vibrato is fine; wobble and drift are not.

Harmonic focus is the share of sound energy that sits exactly on your harmonics, rather than in noise between them. Purity combines all three, 40/30/30.

Tone color shows how strong each harmonic is. A sine-like "oo" is almost all harmonic 1. Bright vowels like "ee" lift the upper bars. A single towering upper bar means you are overtone singing: two notes at once.
